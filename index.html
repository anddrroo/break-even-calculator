How Break-Even & Profit Goals Work

Every meal sold covers its own ingredients and packaging. The remaining cash is your Contribution Margin—the real profit that first pays off your daily overhead bills (break-even), and then goes straight into your pocket as take-home profit:

1. Fixed Overhead Bills you pay even if you sell 0 meals (gas, permits, loan, prep kitchen).
2. Meal Cost What you spend per plate (meat, buns, sauces, boxes, napkins).
3. Contribution Margin Price minus Meal Cost. Every sale pays down bills or builds your profit!
The Calculations:
Break-Even Meals = Daily Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution Margin
Profit Goal Meals = (Daily Fixed Costs + Desired Profit) ÷ Contribution Margin

How Your Numbers Add Up

1
Find your Contribution Margin per meal:
Selling Price minus Cost to Make.
Price - Cost = Margin
2
Calculate meals needed to break even:
Divide daily fixed overhead by your margin per meal.
Fixed Costs ÷ Margin = Break-Even Meals
3
Calculate meals to hit your profit goal:
Add desired profit to fixed overhead, then divide by your margin per meal.
(Fixed Costs + Target Profit) ÷ Margin = Goal Meals
4
Round up to whole meals:
Because you only sell whole portions, you must sell 0 meals to meet your goal.
