How the Break-Even Calculation Works

Every meal you sell brings in money, but part of that money immediately goes toward food ingredients and takeout packaging. The remaining cash is your Contribution Margin—the real profit that chips away at your daily overhead bills until you break even:

1. Fixed Overhead Bills you owe even if you sell 0 meals (gas, permits, loan, prep kitchen).
2. Meal Cost What you spend per plate (meat, buns, sauces, boxes, napkins).
3. Margin per Meal Price minus Meal Cost. Every sale pays down your fixed overhead!
The Break-Even Formula:
Break-Even Meals = Daily Fixed Costs ÷ (Selling Price − Cost to Make One Meal)
